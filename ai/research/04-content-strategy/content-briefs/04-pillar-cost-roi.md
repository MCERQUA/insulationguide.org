Primary: `attic insulation cost` (9,900/mo, $9.94 CPC), `spray foam insulation cost` (6,600,
$5.99), `insulation cost per square foot` (1,000, $4.24), `insulation cost` (2,400, $4.57).
Secondary: `blown in insulation cost` (5,400, $8.06), `fiberglass insulation cost` (4,400,
$4.95), `cellulose insulation cost` (590, $8.94), `insulation installation cost` (590,
$16.08), `cost to insulate a house` (390, $6.02), `crawl space insulation` (14,800, $22.02),
`best insulation for crawl space` (880, $5.73), `best insulation for basement` (320, $3.28),
`attic insulation removal` (1,900, $20.52), `cost to insulate garage` (590, $9.38), `cost to
insulate crawl space` (320, $16.62), `average cost of home insulation` (110, $6.09),
`insulation tax credit` (880, $5.46), `insulation rebate` (390, $8.14), `insulation cost
calculator` (320, $6.63), `insulation contractors near me` (18,100, $18.25 — highest-CPC term
in the research set; woven into the CTA/conclusion, not targeted as a standalone H2).

## Search intent
Mixed informational→commercial (0.72 probability informational on the head term, but this
cluster carries by far the highest CPCs in the entire keyword set — genuine buying-stage
signal). This is the pillar with the most natural, high-density CTA placement.

## Page purpose
The biggest content gap identified in competitor research (see gaps-opportunities.md #4) —
nobody combines cost-per-sq-ft, whole-house cost, DIY-vs-pro cost delta, ROI/payback, and the
federal tax credit in one page. Retailers show list price only; publishers and gov sites show
none of this.

## Outline
1. **Intro** — why insulation cost varies so much (material, R-value target, home size,
   accessibility, removal needs) — sets honest expectations before any numbers.
2. **Cost per square foot by material** (H2) — table: fiberglass, cellulose, blown-in, spray
   foam (open/closed cell), rigid foam, mineral wool — installed cost range per sq ft.
3. **Whole-house / by-room cost estimates** (H2) — attic, walls, crawl space, basement,
   garage — typical project cost ranges for common home sizes, directly answering the PAA
   cluster ("how much is 1000 sq ft of insulation," "2000 sq ft attic cost").
4. **DIY vs. professional cost delta** (H2, short) — materials-only DIY cost vs. installed
   professional cost, bridges to the DIY-vs-Professional pillar for the full decision
   framework (this page covers the $ delta, that page covers the risk/skill delta).
5. **Insulation removal cost** (H2) — old/contaminated insulation removal, a distinct
   high-CPC cluster (`attic insulation removal` $20.52 CPC) — short section, links to
   azinsulationremoval.com as a "learn more" pathway for AZ readers specifically.
6. **ROI, payback period, and energy savings** (H2) — how insulation upgrades pay back via
   lower utility bills, realistic payback-period framing (not oversold).
7. **Federal insulation tax credit & rebates** (H2) — real, current federal incentive info
   (ENERGY STAR is the authoritative source here — cite it), plus a note to check
   utility/state rebates.
8. **What drives your specific quote up or down** (H2) — accessibility, existing insulation
   removal, attic prep, home age/construction — sets up "the only way to know your real number
   is a quote" as the honest bridge into Get Matched.
9. **FAQ block** (schema-ready) — cost PAA cluster.
10. **Get Matched CTA** — the highest-intent placement on the whole site: "See what insulation
    actually costs for your home — get matched with a vetted, insured local contractor for a
    real quote." Should appear after section 8 and again at page end.
11. **Cross-links** to Types, R-Value, DIY-vs-Professional pillars; azinsulationremoval.com
    callout in the removal section.
