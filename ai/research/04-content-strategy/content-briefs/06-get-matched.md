# Content Brief: Get Matched Page

## Target keywords
Primary: `insulation contractors near me` (18,100/mo, $18.25 CPC — highest-value term in the
full research set). Secondary/supporting: `crawl space insulation` and other high-CPC
"ready to hire" terms funnel here from pillar CTAs rather than being targeted directly on this
page (a national page shouldn't chase "near me" phrasing head-on against local-pack results —
see keyword-research.md methodology note).

## Search intent
Commercial/transactional. This is the conversion page — visitor has already decided (or is
close to deciding) they want a professional and is choosing who to trust.

## Page purpose
Convert educated, warmed-up pillar-page traffic (and any direct "insulation contractors near
me" arrivals) into a genuine lead for CCA's vetted, insured contractor-matching service — the
same real capability model as azinsulationremoval.com, not a marketplace-honesty hedge.

## Outline
1. **Hero** — "Get Matched with a Vetted, Insured Insulation Contractor" headline, one-line
   description of how matching works, primary CTA (form/phone).
2. **Why vetted + insured matters** (H2) — directly ties back to the spray-foam-reputation
   trust content from the Types pillar and the "Is Spray Foam Insulation Safe?" blog post:
   installation quality is the real risk variable, which is exactly what vetting solves.
3. **How it works** (H2) — simple 3-step process (tell us about your project → we match you
   with a vetted local contractor → get your quote), sets honest expectations, no
   overselling.
4. **What "vetted and insured" actually means** (H2) — licensing verification, insurance
   verification — concrete trust signals, not vague marketing language (per
   `07-conversion-patterns/`).
5. **Match request form** — project type (attic/wall/crawl space/basement/whole-house),
   material interest (optional — "not sure" as a valid answer, reinforcing the neutral,
   educational brand voice), location, contact info.
6. **Trust/social proof block** — CCA credibility (insurance agency backing, established
   contractor network), consistent with azinsulationremoval.com's real-service framing.
7. **FAQ** — "Does this cost anything?" "How fast will I hear back?" "What if I'm not sure
   what insulation I need?" (answer: that's fine, that's what the guide + matching process is
   for).
8. **Secondary CTA** — phone number, direct-call option for readers who'd rather talk than
   fill a form.

## Word count target
600-900 words of body copy (this is a conversion page, not a content-depth page — keep friction
low).

## Internal links
All four pillars (as the destination they link TO), not really a source of outbound content
links itself.
