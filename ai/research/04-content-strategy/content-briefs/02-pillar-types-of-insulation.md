# Content Brief: Pillar Page — "Types of Insulation Explained"

## Target keywords
Primary: `spray foam insulation` (90,500/mo), `fiberglass insulation` (60,500/mo), `cellulose
insulation` (22,200/mo), `types of insulation` (2,900/mo), `types of home insulation`
(1,000/mo).
Secondary/section targets: `mineral wool insulation` (14,800), `rigid foam insulation`
(8,100), `batt insulation` (18,100), `blown in insulation` (27,100), `radiant barrier
insulation` (5,400), `soundproof insulation` (5,400), `eco friendly insulation` (4,400),
`sheep wool insulation` (5,400), `recycled denim insulation` (1,600), `spray foam vs
fiberglass insulation` (880), `fiberglass vs cellulose insulation` (1,900), `open cell vs
closed cell spray foam` (2,400), `attic insulation types` (2,400), `wall insulation types`
(880).

## Search intent
Overwhelmingly informational (search_intent probability 1.00 on `types of insulation`) —
reader is comparing options, often hasn't decided anything yet. Some sub-terms
(`spray foam insulation`) skew broad enough to include commercial intent for later-funnel
searchers — page should serve both without forcing a sale.

## Page purpose
The primary awareness-stage pillar. Modeled structurally on This Old House's pros/cons format
(best in competitive set — see `03-competitor-analysis/site-reviews/04-thisoldhouse.md`) but
adds real cost signals and climate/use-case framing GBA and NAIMA have but TOH lacks, plus an
honest section addressing the spray-foam-reputation trust questions no competitor tackles
directly.

## Outline
1. **Intro** — "There's no single 'best' insulation" framing (borrowed honestly from NAIMA's
   neutral positioning) — sets expectation that the right material depends on location,
   budget, climate, and DIY appetite.
2. **The main insulation types** (H2 per material, each with a consistent pros/cons format
   like TOH):
   - Fiberglass (batts/rolls + blown-in)
   - Cellulose (blown-in/loose-fill)
   - Spray foam (open-cell vs. closed-cell subsection)
   - Rigid foam board
   - Mineral wool (rock/slag wool)
   Each subsection: what it's made of, R-value per inch, best-suited applications, skill
   level (DIY-friendly / pro-recommended, echoing ENERGY STAR's useful skill-level tagging),
   rough relative cost tier (link to Cost pillar for real numbers), 3 pros / 3 cons.
3. **Specialty/eco-friendly options** (H2) — radiant barrier, sheep wool, recycled denim,
   cotton batts — plain-language treatment of the EPA "greener insulation" criteria (VOCs,
   recycled content) nobody else covers for a homeowner audience. Fills gap #5.
4. **Choosing by location** (H2, subsections: Attic / Walls / Crawl Space / Basement) —
   short guidance per location, linking into the location-specific cost/R-value data on other
   pillars rather than duplicating it.
5. **"Is spray foam insulation safe? Addressing the reputation questions honestly"** (H2) —
   directly answers PAA: why insurance companies scrutinize it, why some buyers walk away from
   homes with it, why removal happens — framed around "installation quality is the real
   variable, not the material itself." Links to the blog post "Is Spray Foam Insulation Safe?"
   for the full treatment and bridges to Get Matched ("this is exactly why vetted, insured
   installers matter").
6. **Acoustic performance / soundproofing** (short H2 or callout box) — plain-language NRC/STC
   translation, links to the "Does Insulation Reduce Noise?" blog post. Fills gap #6.
7. **Comparison table** — quick-reference chart: material, R-value/inch, DIY-friendly?,
   relative cost, best for. (This is the single-page "everything in one place" competitors
   don't offer.)
8. **FAQ block** (schema-ready) — PAA-sourced: "What is the best insulation for an attic?"
   "Is spray foam insulation worth it?" etc.
9. **Get Matched CTA** (after the reputation-honesty section and again at page end).
10. **Cross-links** to R-Value, Cost & ROI, DIY vs. Professional pillars + future-linking
    callout to sprayfoamscience.com for deep spray-foam building science.
