# Content Brief: About Page

## Target keywords
Low-volume/brand/navigational — this page is not a primary SEO target. Its job is trust
reinforcement for visitors who click through to verify who's behind the site before filling
out the Get Matched form.

## Search intent
Navigational/trust-verification.

## Page purpose
Explain who runs InsulationGuide (Contractors Choice Agency) and why that matters — CCA is a
real, established agency, not an anonymous content farm or a single contractor with a
conflict of interest. Reinforces the neutral-guide brand promise made throughout the pillar
content.

## Outline
1. **Who we are** (H2) — InsulationGuide is a resource from Contractors Choice Agency (CCA),
   an established agency that also operates a network of specialized insulation-industry
   properties (sprayfoamscience.com, foammedic.com, azinsulationremoval.com,
   insulationcertificate.com) — real breadth of insulation-industry involvement, not a
   single-contractor site.
2. **Why we built this guide** (H2) — the genuine "start here, get educated, then get matched
   with someone vetted" mission — no product to sell, no single material to push.
3. **How we stay neutral** (H2) — explains the business model plainly: CCA earns by
   connecting homeowners with vetted, insured contractors, not by selling insulation
   materials — which is why the guide can afford to be honest about pros/cons rather than
   steering toward one brand or material.
4. **Contact info block** — phone (844-967-5247), email (josh@contractorschoiceagency.com),
   HQ (Chandler, AZ) — same canonical contact info as Contact page.
5. **Get Matched CTA** (soft, secondary placement).
