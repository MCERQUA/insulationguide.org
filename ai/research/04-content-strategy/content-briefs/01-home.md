# Content Brief: Home Page

## Target keywords
Primary: `types of insulation` (2,900/mo, HIGH comp), brand/generic "insulation guide."
Secondary: light touches of `insulation r value`, `insulation cost`, `diy insulation` as
teaser copy for the four pillar cards.

## Search intent
Navigational/informational — visitor may arrive via brand search, direct pillar-topic search
that ranked the homepage secondarily, or internal/external referral. Job of the page is to
instantly communicate "start here" and route to the right pillar fast.

## Page purpose
Hub and trust-establisher. Not a ranking play in itself beyond broad brand terms — its job is
conversion-of-attention: get the visitor to the right pillar page or straight to Get Matched
if they already know what they want.

## Outline
1. **Hero** — warm-earth-tone visual, headline framing the site as a genuine "start here"
   guide (not a sales pitch), subhead naming the four core questions homeowners have
   (what type, how much R-value, what will it cost, DIY or hire someone). Primary CTA:
   "Get Matched with a Vetted, Insured Insulation Contractor" as secondary hero CTA (not the
   only CTA — education-first framing per brand tone).
2. **"Where do you want to start?"** — four cards linking to the four pillars, each with a
   one-line teaser and the real question it answers (e.g., "Not sure fiberglass vs. spray
   foam vs. cellulose is right for you? Start here.")
3. **"Why trust this guide"** — short trust block: backed by Contractors Choice Agency,
   genuinely neutral (no single material pushed), real contractor-matching capability,
   licensed/insured vetting standard.
4. **Quick-answer strip** — 3-4 bite-size facts (e.g., "Most homes need R-38 to R-60 in the
   attic," "Spray foam costs 2-3x more than fiberglass but seals air leaks fiberglass can't")
   with links into the relevant pillar section — SEO value via internal anchor-text variety.
5. **"Learn more from CCA's insulation specialists"** — light-touch cross-property teaser
   (see `05-topical-map/`) noting sprayfoamscience.com/foammedic.com/azinsulationremoval.com
   exist for readers who've narrowed their focus — framed as "further reading," not a hard
   nav item yet.
6. **Get Matched CTA block** (secondary placement, mid/lower page) — full CTA with trust
   signals (vetted, insured) per `07-conversion-patterns/`.
7. **Footer** — standard nav, About/Contact, phone/email.

## Word count target
600-900 words of actual body copy (this is a hub page, not a content-depth play — keep it
scannable).

## Internal links
All four pillars, Get Matched, About. Optional: blog hub.

## Meta
- Title: "Insulation Guide: Types, R-Value, Cost & DIY vs. Professional | InsulationGuide"
- Description: "A genuinely neutral, start-here guide to home insulation — compare types,
  understand R-value, see real cost ranges, and decide DIY vs. hiring a pro. Get matched with
  a vetted, insured insulation contractor when you're ready."
