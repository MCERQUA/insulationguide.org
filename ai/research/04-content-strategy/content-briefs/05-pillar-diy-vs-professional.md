## Search intent
Informational (0.98 probability on `diy insulation`) but with a real commercial undertone —
several near-zero-volume terms here carry outsized CPCs ($24.90 on `can you install insulation
yourself`), suggesting readers arriving at "can I do this myself" are often one honest answer
away from deciding to hire someone.

## Page purpose
The genuinely neutral decision-framework page retailers structurally can't offer (their
guides always end at "add to cart," never "actually, hire a pro for this one"). Borrows This
Old House's install-method section and extends it into a full decision framework.

## Outline
1. **Intro** — reframe the question as "which parts of this job are DIY-appropriate," not a
   binary — sets neutral tone immediately.
2. **What you can realistically DIY** (H2) — fiberglass batts, some rigid foam board, some
   loose-fill with a rented blower — what makes these DIY-friendly (accessibility, low
   hazard, forgiving of minor mistakes).
3. **What's professional-only (and why)** (H2) — spray foam (mixing/off-gassing risk,
   equipment cost, ventilation requirements), attic blown-in at scale, crawl space
   encapsulation (moisture/mold risk if done wrong) — honest risk explanation, not scare
   tactics.
4. **Step-by-step: DIY attic insulation with batts** (H2) — genuinely useful how-to (borrowing
   the practical-instruction strength of the retailer guides) — tools, safety gear, common
   mistakes.
5. **Step-by-step: DIY garage insulation** (H2) — shorter, same pattern; garage is a common
   approachable DIY project.
6. **Is DIY spray foam safe?** (H2) — directly answers the PAA — explains why spray foam kits
   exist but carry real risk (improper mixing ratios cause off-gassing, structural/ventilation
   issues), and why this is the single clearest case for professional installation. Links to
   the "Is Spray Foam Insulation Safe?" blog post for the fuller reputation/trust discussion.
7. **DIY cost savings vs. the real risk of mistakes** (H2) — under-insulating, moisture
   trapping, fire-safety/clearance errors, voided warranties — the honest cost of getting it
   wrong, which is what ultimately justifies professional installation for higher-risk jobs.
8. **How to tell your project needs a pro** (H2) — decision checklist (spray foam? crawl
   space? whole-house? not comfortable on a ladder in an attic for hours? → hire a pro).
9. **FAQ block** (schema-ready) — PAA cluster: best way to install, easiest type to DIY, best
   DIY insulation.
10. **Get Matched CTA** — placed right after section 8's checklist: "If your project checked
    any of those boxes, get matched with a vetted, insured insulation contractor near you."
11. **Cross-links** to Types (material DIY-friendliness already flagged there), Cost & ROI
    (DIY-vs-pro cost delta section).
