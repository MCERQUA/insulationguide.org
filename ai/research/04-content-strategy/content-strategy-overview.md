Contact, and Blog (hub + 4 post briefs). This file is the map/rationale; each brief has full
detail (target keywords, outline, word count, CTA placement, internal links).

## Content principles (from `03-competitor-analysis/gaps-opportunities.md`)

1. **Completeness in one page** — each pillar combines what competitors scatter across many
   URLs (type + application + cost + skill level in Types; R-value + climate-zone table in
   R-Value; cost-per-sqft + whole-house + ROI + tax credit in Cost).
2. **Honest neutrality** — no single "best" material; follow NAIMA's "installation quality
   matters more than material" framing, echoed from This Old House's approachable tone.
3. **Address trust concerns directly** — the spray-foam-reputation PAA cluster gets a real,
   honest section, not avoidance.
4. **Real next step** — Get Matched CTA appears at natural decision points (end of pillar
   sections, not interrupting early education), consistent with `07-conversion-patterns/`.
5. **Future-linking to CCA's niche sites** — sprayfoamscience.com, foammedic.com,
   azinsulationremoval.com referenced as "learn more" pathways once a reader narrows interest
   (see `05-topical-map/`), even where not all links are live yet.

## Blog post topics selected (4)

1. **"Eco-Friendly Insulation Options: Sheep Wool, Recycled Denim & Other Sustainable
   Choices"** — targets `eco friendly insulation` (4,400/mo), `sheep wool insulation`
   (5,400/mo), `recycled denim insulation` (1,600/mo). Fills gap #5 (nobody serves this
   consumer-friendly).
2. **"Does Insulation Reduce Noise? A Homeowner's Guide to Soundproofing"** — targets
   `soundproof insulation` (5,400/mo), `best insulation for soundproofing walls` (720/mo).
   Fills gap #6.
3. **"Signs You Need More Insulation (and How Much You Actually Need)"** — targets `how much
   insulation do i need` (1,300/mo) plus PAA-adjacent "signs you need more insulation."
   Awareness-stage diagnostic angle, feeds the R-Value and Cost pillars.
4. **"Is Spray Foam Insulation Safe? What Homeowners Should Know Before They Buy"** —
   targets `spray foam insulation pros and cons` (590/mo), `is spray foam insulation worth
   it` (170/mo), and directly answers the PAA trust cluster ("why do insurance companies not
   like spray foam," "why are buyers walking away from homes with spray foam"). Fills gap #3.

Each addresses a real content gap identified in competitor research, uses real (mostly
low-competition) search volume, and links back into at least two of the four pillar pages.
