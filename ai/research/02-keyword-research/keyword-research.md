## Notes / methodology

- Location: United States (national — this site is not geo-targeted like azinsulationremoval.com).
- Some long-tail phrases (climate-zone state combos, "insulate crawl space vs encapsulate",
  "vapor barrier vs insulation") returned no recorded Google Ads volume — this is common for
  genuinely long-tail informational queries and does NOT mean zero search interest; they are
  retained as section-level/FAQ targets rather than page-level targets, consistent with PAA
  evidence that the underlying questions are real.
- `insulation contractors near me` and `crawl space insulation` are geo-modified/commercial
  terms with volume attributed at the national level by DataForSEO's aggregation — actual local
  intent is baked in per-searcher location. Treat as strong CTA/funnel anchors, not pillar-page
  targets (a national educational hub shouldn't try to out-rank local contractor sites on
  "near me" phrasing directly).
