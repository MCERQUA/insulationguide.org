# Competitor Review: GreenBuildingAdvisor — Insulation Overview (greenbuildingadvisor.com)

**URL reviewed:** greenbuildingadvisor.com/green-basics/insulation-overview-2 (+ Insulation
collection hub, Fibrous Insulation Types article)
**Type:** Building-science publisher / paid-membership content site (GBA Prime)
**Domain authority:** High within the building-science/DIY-builder niche

## Structure
- "Green Basics" primer with Overview/Details tab split
- Covers the three heat-transfer mechanisms (conduction, convection, radiation) before even
  getting to material types — genuinely teaches the "why" behind insulation
- R-value + material comparison, thermal bridging, air sealing/moisture control woven together
  rather than siloed
- DOE climate-zone R-value table
- Comparative lists ranking materials by air-stopping ability and vapor permeability
- Deep-linked to a large content library (Rigid Foam, Spray Foam, Blown-In dedicated articles)
- Active Q&A community + comment threads under articles

## Strengths
- The most technically rigorous, evidence-based content in the competitive set — building
  science, not marketing
- Genuinely explains *why* (thermal bridging, convective loops, vapor permeability) rather
  than just listing materials
- DOE climate-zone table integrated directly into the overview (not a separate buried page)
- Real community engagement (Q&A, comments) signals ongoing trust/authority

## Weaknesses / gaps
- **Technical level is too high for true awareness-stage/entry-level homeowners** — assumes
  building-science literacy (own profile: this reader is often a total beginner)
- Core content increasingly gated behind GBA Prime membership paywall
- No cost/pricing content at all (this is a design-standards audience, not a "what will this
  cost me" audience)
- No local contractor-matching or install-help pathway — pure content/community, no funnel
- Aesthetically dated, dense text-heavy pages; not scannable for a skimming homeowner

## Tone
Expert-to-expert, precise, unapologetically technical. Assumes the reader wants to get it
right, not get a quick answer.

## Takeaway for insulationguide.org
GBA proves there's real appetite for building-science accuracy (thermal bridging, vapor
permeability, climate-zone tables) but serves it in a way that's inaccessible to true
beginners and paywalls its depth. Our opportunity: borrow GBA's *accuracy* (correct R-value
per inch, real climate-zone data, honest material trade-offs) but present it at a genuinely
entry-level reading level with no paywall, then bridge straight to "so which one and who
installs it" — something GBA structurally can't do.
