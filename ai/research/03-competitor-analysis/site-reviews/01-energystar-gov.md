# Competitor Review: ENERGY STAR — Insulation Guidance (energystar.gov)

**URL reviewed:** energystar.gov/saveathome/seal_insulate/certified_insulation (+ related
Seal & Insulate section, Insulation Tax Credit page)
**Type:** Federal government program site (joint EPA/DOE brand)
**Domain authority:** Very high (.gov-tier trust, near-universal recognition of the blue
ENERGY STAR mark)

## Structure
- Breadcrumb-driven hierarchy under "Save at Home > Seal & Insulate"
- Intro block explaining certification standards (third-party tested, flame resistance)
- Large visual comparison chart of insulation types
- Six insulation categories broken into: Materials / Best Suited For / Skill Level
  (Blanket batts/rolls, Foam board/rigid board, Loose-fill/blown-in, Rigid fibrous,
  Sprayed foam/foamed-in-place)
- Skill-level classification (Basic → Advanced) doubles as an implicit DIY-vs-pro signal
- Separate dedicated page for the federal Insulation Tax Credit
- Embedded "Product Finder" tool for certified products

## Strengths
- Maximum trust/authority — the ENERGY STAR mark itself is the credibility asset
- Clean, scannable type-comparison chart
- Ties directly into the federal tax credit (real financial incentive, own page)
- Skill-level tagging is a genuinely useful buying signal most competitors skip

## Weaknesses / gaps
- **No pricing information anywhere** — a program site can't/won't talk dollars
- **No R-value guidance on this specific page** (lives elsewhere on the site, disconnected)
- **No regional/climate-zone recommendations** on this page
- Zero personality/brand voice — purely reference material, not a "guide" a reader browses
  end-to-end
- No path to actually hiring anyone — it's a tool/certification resource, not a funnel
- Content is scattered across many separate URLs rather than living in one cohesive pillar

## Tone
Informative, authoritative, certification-first. Reads like a spec sheet, not a story.

## Takeaway for insulationguide.org
ENERGY STAR wins on trust and the skill-level/DIY signal but loses on completeness (no cost,
no climate guidance in one place) and has zero commercial funnel. A single pillar page that
combines type comparison + skill level + R-value + climate-zone guidance + real cost ranges
+ a genuine next step (Get Matched) beats this by being the "one page that has everything,"
while still citing ENERGY STAR/DOE data as the authority backing our numbers.
