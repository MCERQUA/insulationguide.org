The DIY vs Pro Delta

While purchasing materials yourself can seem cost-effective, professional installation labor typically adds $40 to $80 per hour to the project cost.

info

When DIY makes sense:

Laying fiberglass batts in an easily accessible, unfinished open attic.

warning

When to hire a Pro:

Spray foam application, existing wall cavities, or handling older materials (potential asbestos).
